## Reference outcome

Target family: deep melodic house / melodic techno / cinematic progressive electronic performance.

This is a workflow and quality reference, not an instruction to copy any artist's copyrighted track or proprietary preset.

Useful reference behaviors:
- Hozho: dark melodic identity, strong bass/synth palette, dramatic space, distinct sound design.
- Cassian: simple but carefully controlled low end; kick/sub translation; hardware-like synth character; Ableton-centered live triggering/effects.
- Agents of Time: studio-to-stage arrangement, Ableton live set, hardware/synth/pad/FX integration, finished mix/master before performance.
- professional DJ/live systems: multiple musical sources routed through a mixer where filters, dynamics, sends, loops and transitions reshape complete musical material.

## 30-second acceptance path

### 0-4 seconds: immediate identity
PLAY begins with a complete foundation:
- convincing kick;
- low-end bed or bass pulse;
- atmosphere;
- harmonic context.

No isolated "bing" note is allowed to define the first impression.

### 4-12 seconds: groove establishes
- kick and bass lock together;
- percussion adds movement without sounding like a metronome;
- chord/pad layer creates width and emotional center;
- no role masks another.

### 12-20 seconds: phrase develops
- melody/pluck phrase has a beginning, development and return;
- bass pattern relates to harmony;
- at least one automation movement creates evolution;
- atmosphere remains textural rather than constant noise.

### 20-26 seconds: tension
BUILD or LIFT must:
- reduce or reshape low end;
- increase spectral movement/space;
- add rising/tension material;
- create obvious expectation of a release.

### 26-30 seconds: payoff
DROP/PEAK must:
- restore/strengthen kick and bass;
- change energy clearly;
- include impact/transient punctuation;
- feel louder/denser through arrangement and dynamics, not clipping.

## Role acceptance

### KICK
PASS if:
- solid fundamental with a controlled transient;
- consistent level;
- mono-compatible low end;
- does not ring like a toy membrane synth.

FAIL if:
- clicky, papery, boingy, or obviously synthetic in a cheap way.

### BASS
PASS if:
- contains sub plus audible character;
- groove interacts with kick;
- pattern changes with harmony/phrase;
- low end remains controlled.

FAIL if:
- single repeating note with no phrase relationship.

### PERCUSSION
PASS if:
- hats/claps/shakers/toms produce groove and fills;
- velocity/timing variation prevents machine-gun repetition.

FAIL if:
- identical tick every subdivision.

### CHORDS / PAD
PASS if:
- harmony has voicing/inversion/movement;
- width and reverb create depth without washing out the mix;
- chord changes support the bass.

FAIL if:
- one block chord repeated every bar with identical envelope.

### MELODY
PASS if:
- motif is phrase-based, with rests and variation;
- note selection stays in harmonic context;
- phrase evolves over 4/8/16 bars.

FAIL if:
- random scale notes or repeated arpeggio with no shape.

### ATMOSPHERE
PASS if:
- movement feels like air, ocean, crowd-space, field recording, noise bed, or evolving texture;
- level and filter evolve.

FAIL if:
- static pink noise dominates the experience.

### VOCAL
PASS if:
- actual human vocal sample/recording or convincing processed voice texture;
- used sparingly as a phrase/texture;
- delay/reverb/filter treatment integrates it musically.

FAIL if:
- oscillator pretending to be a vocal.

### FX / TRANSITIONS
PASS if:
- risers, impacts, reverses, echoes, filter moves and reverb throws are tied to arrangement boundaries;
- BUILD/DROP cause musically meaningful transitions.

FAIL if:
- FX are merely toggled wet/dry with no phrase timing.

## Mix acceptance

- kick and sub are centered and controlled;
- no accidental clipping;
- master dynamics provide glue, not pumping chaos;
- stereo width is mostly above low bass;
- reverb tails do not mask the next phrase;
- perceived level remains stable across scenes;
- phone speaker remains intelligible;
- headphones reveal low-end depth and width;
- no harsh continuous highs.

## Interaction acceptance

Every primary control must satisfy:

user action -> visible state -> obvious musical consequence -> reversible behavior

Examples:
- KICK off: groove loses kick immediately/quantized at boundary.
- BREAK: low end falls away and space/vocal/melody becomes dominant.
- BUILD: tension rises over a known duration.
- DROP: energy returns on a musical boundary.
- WASH: temporary spatial bloom, then recoverable.
- FUCK IT: produces a new musically coherent variant, not random noise.
